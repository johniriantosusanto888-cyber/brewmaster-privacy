Delete Your BrewMaster Account

To delete your account and all associated data from BrewMaster:

  1. Open the BrewMaster app
  2. Go to the Sync tab
  3. Tap Sign Out
  4. Your cloud sync data will be disconnected from your account

To request complete deletion of your data from our servers, email us at:
johniriantosusanto@gmail.com
with subject: "BrewMaster Account Deletion Request"

We will process your request within 7 business days.

Data that will be deleted:

  • Your Google account email associated with BrewMaster sync
  • All synced recipes stored in our cloud

Note: Local data on your device can be removed by uninstalling the app.
